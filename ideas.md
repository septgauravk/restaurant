# Design brainstorm — Restaurant AI Image Opportunity Research

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Warm Operator Dashboard
Very Brief Intro: A practical, editorial research report with saffron, cream and deep green, designed to feel like a smart business briefing rather than a glossy marketing site.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Market Signal / Field Notes
Very Brief Intro: A high-contrast editorial layout with bold data marks, red annotations and paper-like surfaces, evoking a strategist’s annotated field notebook.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Quiet Commerce
Very Brief Intro: A restrained, premium light interface with charcoal typography, citrus accents and modular evidence cards for founders who want clarity over hype.
Probability: 0.01

## Chosen approach: Market Signal / Field Notes

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial information design influenced by Swiss International Typographic Style and Indian vernacular print ephemera: clear hierarchy, strong grids used as scaffolding, and expressive annotation marks that make evidence feel tangible.

### Core Principles
1. Every claim is paired with a source, caveat or measurable next step.
2. Use asymmetric editorial composition: a persistent rail, offset panels and data cards rather than one centered marketing stack.
3. Warm, high-contrast surfaces should feel like a working research desk: cream paper, charcoal ink, vermilion signal marks and muted green validation cues.
4. Motion stays purposeful: charts animate softly, controls respond crisply, and no ornamental effects slow down a mobile-first reading experience.

### Color Philosophy
The signature color is **signal vermilion #E6532F**: it calls attention to decisions and risk without looking like a generic startup orange. It sits on parchment **#F7F0E5**, ink **#202321**, and validation green **#2F6B57**. Yellow is reserved for uncertainty and assumptions. The emotional intent is pragmatic confidence: “we can act, but we will not overclaim.”

### Layout Paradigm
A two-axis research desk: a narrow left rail names the report and section, while the main canvas moves between an evidence-led opener, a horizontal signal band, asymmetric cards, and a wide recommendation matrix. On mobile the rail becomes a compact sticky index.

### Signature Elements
- Small uppercase “FIELD NOTE” labels with red index numbers.
- Evidence cards with a colored left rule, source chip and confidence marker.
- Chart captions that explicitly distinguish “observed,” “reported,” and “hypothesis.”

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like highlighting a briefing: clicking a chart filter changes one clear dimension, tabs reveal the underlying evidence, and copy/share controls confirm completion with small inline feedback. No hidden complexity.

### Animation
Use 180–240ms ease-out transitions. Chart bars rise once on load; cards fade in with a 40ms stagger only when motion is allowed. Hover states shift color and translate the signal marker by 2px. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep keyboard focus highly visible.

### Typography System
Display: **Space Grotesk** 700/600 for compact, analytical headlines. Body: **DM Sans** 400/500 for approachable reading. Metadata: Space Grotesk 600 in uppercase with tracking. Hierarchy: 56px desktop report title, 36px section titles, 18px lead, 15px body, 12px metadata; scale down smoothly on mobile.

### Brand Essence
A research desk for turning a restaurant visual problem into a testable product — for practical founders building in India, without marketing fluff. Personality: **clear, grounded, incisive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are specific and slightly provocative. CTAs are action-oriented but never inflated. Microcopy admits uncertainty and tells the reader what to do next.
Example lines: “The opportunity is real. The promise needs guardrails.” / “Turn the insight into a 14-day test.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact mark made from two offset rectangular “plates” and a single vermilion registration dot, suggesting a food image frame plus a printer’s crop mark. Use the mark without text as the favicon; pair it with a custom-spaced “PLATE / SIGNAL” wordmark.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Vermilion — #E6532F**

## Style Decisions
Use generated food imagery sparingly as a visual anchor, not as fake proof. Label examples as illustrative. Keep the report credible by showing source notes, confidence tags, and explicit hypotheses. Avoid testimonials, fabricated ratings and invented customer quotes.
