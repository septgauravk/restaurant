# Plate / Signal — Business opportunity & product strategy

## Cover
Plate / Signal
The restaurant image gap
Business opportunity and product strategy for an AI-assisted visual merchandising workflow in India
Manus AI · September 2026

## Slide 1
The opportunity is real. The promise needs guardrails.

- Independent restaurant owners need better visual merchandising across Google, delivery listings and social.
- A prompt pack can reduce the friction and recurring cost of image production.
- The product must start with the real dish and avoid promising rankings or guaranteed orders.

## Slide 2
India’s food-service market is large enough to test seriously.

- USDA FAS estimates India’s food-service industry at $85B in 2025.
- The same report projects $140B by 2030, implying a 10.4% CAGR.
- More than 500,000 restaurant outlets are reported across formats.
- These figures indicate addressable market scale—not validated demand for this specific product.

Source: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service, India HRI Annual 2025 [1]

## Slide 3
Restaurant discovery is already a multi-surface visual problem.

- Google Business Profile connects photos and video with menus, ordering links, reviews and performance insights.
- Delivery listings place dish imagery beside nearby alternatives.
- Instagram and WhatsApp extend the same visual asset into discovery and follow-up.
- One image is not enough: each channel has a different job and crop.

Sources: Google Business Profile restaurant guide [2]; Google photo quality guidance [3]

## Slide 4
The customer pain is practical, recurring and easy to recognize.

- Phone photos often look dull, inconsistent or amateur.
- Professional shoots are expensive to repeat whenever a menu changes.
- Owners need assets for menus, profiles, delivery platforms and social posts.
- The real job is not “make an AI picture”; it is “make my real dish easier to sell visually.”

## Slide 5
The defensible product is a workflow, not a magic image.

1. Start with a real dish photo or clear dish description.
2. Apply cuisine-specific prompt recipes, lighting direction and consistency rules.
3. Export channel-ready variations for menu, profile, delivery and social.
4. Measure usability, time saved and downstream profile actions.

## Slide 6
A low-ticket entry product can open a higher-value service ladder.

- Prompt Pack: ₹299–499 for owners who want a do-it-yourself system.
- Setup Service: prepare 10 signature dishes with consistent visual direction.
- Consistency Kit: brand style, background rules and crop templates.
- Pilot Review: compare baseline versus assisted workflow over 14 days.

Positioning: affordable enough to try; structured enough to become a repeatable operating system.

## Slide 7
The first launch should measure usefulness before claiming growth.

Pilot design:

- 10 restaurants across at least two city tiers and three cuisine types.
- 7-day baseline followed by 7 days using the prompt pack and image checklist.
- Capture four signals: menu clicks, profile actions, inquiries and time to first usable image.
- Log failure modes: dish fidelity, artifacts, editing time and tool availability.

This turns a marketing claim into a testable hypothesis.

## Slide 8
Trust is part of the product experience.

- Google’s guidance says restaurant photos should be in focus, well lit and representative of reality.
- AI-enhanced images should not materially misrepresent portion, ingredients or availability.
- Do not claim better Google ranking, guaranteed orders or unlimited access to a free tool.
- Keep the workflow model-agnostic because Google’s image tools and terms can change.

Sources: Google photo guidance [3]; Google Imagen documentation [5]

## Slide 9
The simplest fulfillment stack is payment plus controlled delivery.

- Razorpay Payment Links can accept payments without a website and support UPI, cards, netbanking and other methods.
- Payment success should trigger a webhook or automation step.
- Deliver a unique, expiring download link by email and WhatsApp where consent exists.
- Avoid a permanent public Google Drive link for the master PDF.

Source: Razorpay Payment Links documentation [4]

## Slide 10
Recommended next move: sell 10 pilot slots, not a broad launch.

- Recruit owners through WhatsApp, local restaurant networks and direct outreach.
- Offer a clear deliverable: one prompt system, 10 dish workflows and channel-ready exports.
- Ask for permission to measure usage and outcomes; do not fabricate testimonials.
- Use pilot evidence to decide whether the next investment is acquisition, service delivery or productization.

The decision to make next is simple: do owners get a usable image faster—and will they pay to repeat the process?
